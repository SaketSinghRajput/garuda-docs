26 August 2025

Main dheere dheere door nahi hua.

Main gayab ho gaya. Har darwaaza band karke
tumhein khaamoshi mein chhod diya.

Sach jo tumhein tab bata dena chahiye tha:
tumse kuch mahine pehle ek rishta toota tha, aur main abhi bhi apne shak mein khoya hua tha. Jab main tumhare liye girne laga, main dar gaya — aur khud ko yakeen dilaya ki chhod dena tumhein bacha lega.

Aisa nahi hua. Darr bata sakta hai ki main kyun bhaaga.
Par ye kabhi jayaz nahi karta ki maine kaise kiya.

Tum ek wajah aur ek alvida ke haqdaar the.
Maine dono nahi diye.

Tum ek subah utthi — aur kuch nahi tha.
Wahi khaamoshi hai jisne tumhein toda.

Wo tumhari galti nahi thi. Wo meri thi.

The letter

Mishra Ji,

Main jaanta hoon ek website tumhare zakham nahi bhar sakti. Main ye isliye nahi bana raha ki tum haan bolo. Main isliye bana raha hoon kyunki jo maine kabhi kaha nahi, wo tumhare saamne rakhna chahta hoon.

Maine tumhe khoya nahi — maine tumhe chhoda.
Aur us farq ko main roz jeeta hoon.

Tujhe khoya nahi, main khud se haar gaya,
jo darr tha, use tere naam pe waar gaya.
Ab samajhta hoon — jise chhoda, wahi meri duniya thi,
har raat teri kami mujhe tera pata de gaya.

Jab bhi zindagi mushkil hui, maine tumhein door kiya. Ab main ulta karunga — mushkil mein tumhein paas rakhunga. Tumhein apne bure waqt ka partner banaunga. Tumhein bachane ke naam par phir kabhi gayab nahi hounga.

Aur agar tum mujhe maaf na bhi kar pao —
tab bhi mujhe sukoon hai ki maine tumhe sach bataya.

— tumhara Chomu

Dawn

Main tumse aaj koi faisla nahi maang raha.

Sabse pehle, mujhse pehle — bas khush ho jao.
Dil ki sabse gehri jagah se. Tum iske haqdaar ho,
chahe main is kahani mein hoon ya nahi.

Aur ye meri aakhri promise hai:
agar tum kabhi mujhe haan na bhi bolo —
main phir bhi zindagi bhar tumhein tumhare sapno ki taraf dhakelta rahunga.

Isi se pata chalta hai ki ye tumhein wapas "chahiye" nahi hai.
Ye tumse pyaar hai — darwaaza poora khula rakh ke.

Jab bhi tum ready ho. Agar kabhi ho.
